1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This memo summarizes the findings of the Q3 2026 internal expense
audit and reiterates key expense-reporting rules ahead of Q4
close.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AUDIT SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The Internal Audit team reviewed a sample of 250 expense claims
submitted between July 1 and September 30, 2026, across the
Sales, Engineering, Marketing, and Operations departments,
covering travel, meals, software subscriptions, and client
entertainment categories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- 92% of claims were submitted with itemized receipts, in line
  with policy (target: 95%).
- 14 claims (5.6%) exceeded the daily meal per-diem limit of $75
  without a documented exception approval.
- 6 claims (2.4%) were submitted more than 45 days after the
  expense date, exceeding the reimbursement window.
- Corporate card reconciliations for the quarter were completed
  on time by all departments except Marketing, which closed 4
  business days late.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXPENSE POLICY REMINDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- All claims over $25 require an itemized receipt (credit card
  slips alone are not sufficient).
- Claims must be submitted within 45 calendar days of the expense
  date; late claims require VP-level approval.
- Daily meal per-diem cap: $75 (domestic), $100 (international).
  Exceptions require prior written approval from a department
  head.
- Client entertainment expenses must include attendee names,
  company affiliation, and business purpose.
- Personal expenses (entertainment, dry cleaning under 5-day
  trips, gym fees) remain non-reimbursable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ACTION ITEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Department heads must review flagged claims and respond to
  Internal Audit by October 20, 2026.
- Marketing to confirm a corrective process for on-time card
  reconciliation before Q4 close (December 31, 2026).
- All staff are encouraged to complete the 15-minute "Expense
  Policy Refresher" module in the Learning Portal by
  November 1, 2026.
